### Question 1: How many relations does the Car dealership Schema contain?

The schema contains **3 relations** (tables):
* **CAR**
* **SALE**
* **SALESPERSON**

---

### Question 2: How many columns does the relation Car contain?

The `CAR` relation contains **4 columns** (attributes):
* `Serial no`
* `Model`
* `Manufacturer`
* `Price`

---

### Question 3: What is the degree of the relation Salesperson?

**Answer:**  
The degree of a relation is the total number of columns it contains. For this specific `SALESPERSON` relation, the **degree is 3**. It consists of the following attributes:
* `Salesperson_id`
* `Name`
* `Phone`
